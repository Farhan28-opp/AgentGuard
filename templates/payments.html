How this payment is protected

Direct payment · Recharge, Bills, Send Money · authorized by you

  1. AgentGuard checks your per-payment limit, your wallet key and a behavioural risk pre-check. Nothing is issued yet.
  2. You review and authorize the exact amount.
  3. A single-use mandate for exactly that amount is issued; your wallet key signs the reserve and AgentGuard re-runs the authority and risk checks.
  4. With ledger enforcement on, Drunix must commit the Reserve and the Commit VALID; it settles on the simulated rail with a receipt.

Agentic payment · Shopping

  1. Your Main Agent delegates bounded authority to a Purchase Agent.
  2. The agent signs; AgentGuard checks identity, policy and risk, then holds the amount.
  3. With ledger enforcement on, Drunix must commit the reserve and the commit VALID.
  4. You approve before anything is paid, unless your policy says otherwise.
