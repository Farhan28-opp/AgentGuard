04

Security Lab — ledger bypass

Direct to Drunix · no AgentGuard checks

Each attack is sent straight to the agentauth chaincode through the bridge, with the same ledger identity AgentGuard uses — as if AgentGuard's own backend were compromised. Signatures, policy, the risk engine and PostgreSQL are not involved. The lab uses its own on-chain authority tree (₹10,000 lab mandate); your data is not touched. Each transaction takes about 2 s (Drunix block time).
